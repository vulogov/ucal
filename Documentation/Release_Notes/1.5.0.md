# 1.5.0 — UNRELEASED

In development on the `1.5.0` branch. `main` carries the released 1.4.0 line.

## The goal of this cycle: spend what the research found

1.4.0 was a research cycle and it produced more than it built. Three findings
came with a recommendation attached, and none of them has been acted on:

- **W4 step 1** placed every unit of every body on the universal ladder and
  found Earth's day and Mars's sol on the same rung, 591 and 607, where the
  steps are a factor of 3125. Its recommendation was explicit: do not build the
  two-ladder view, **keep the table** — as a row or two in `ucal cal show`.
- **X1.3** landed a body-file loader and a `cal derive` that stops at
  intercalation, because §15.1's *other* file — the anchor — is still compiled
  in. A derived calendar without a phase cannot produce a date, which is the
  thing a calendar is for.
- **The event catalogue** doubled and now reaches T31. Seven derived calendars
  do not: the mechanism is body-independent and the catalogue of bodies is the
  thinnest part of the argument it makes.

None of this is new design. It is finishing what the last cycle established, and
the cycle is deliberately small.

### Scope

**Y1 — the ladder placement, in `cal show`.** Where this body's day and year sit
on the universal grid, and how far above the rung. W4's kept recommendation,
implemented at the size W4 recommended: rows, not a view.

**Y2 — anchor files.** §15.1 names body files *and* anchor files, versioned
independently because parameters change with better measurement and anchors with
re-determination. 1.4.0 did the first. With the second, `ucal cal derive
--anchor <file>` produces **local fields for a body that does not ship** — a
date, not just an intercalation rule.

This is where the temptation lives, and Rule J is the answer: an anchor is
determined and cited, never guessed. A file must carry the same obligations the
compiled-in anchors do — a phase definition that is a body event, a determination,
a citation, a window containing its own tick — and `Anchor::new` already refuses
anything less. The loader must not become the place that stops asking.

**Y3 — more bodies.** The Galilean moons and Enceladus, cited, most of them
landing where Titan is: complete in units, intercalation and cycles, incomplete
in phase. That is the ordinary case and a `cal list` that shows it eleven times
makes the argument better than one that shows it five.

### Done when

Y1 and Y2 have landed, and Y3 has landed or been recorded as dropped with a
reason. A body that does not ship can be given an anchor from a file and asked
for a date.

### Explicitly not in scope

- **Anything breaking.** `cargo semver-checks` runs against the published 1.4.0.
  The four items in [`ROAD-TO-2.0.md`](../Proposals/ROAD-TO-2.0.md) stay there.
- **Moving the loader into `ucal-body`.** That is 2.0's, for the reason D-A20
  gives: the data model is `&'static str` and a library loader would leak.
- **W3, the third locale**, postponed in 1.4.0 pending a choice of language,
  which decides what the probe tests.

## Carried forward from 1.4.0

- **The three asks in [`CONTACT.md`](../CONTACT.md)** are open, wanted, and — as
  of 1.4.0 — a condition on nothing shipping.
- **The 2.0 bundle**, decided in `ROAD-TO-2.0.md` and not scheduled: withdraw
  `bigint` from the public feature surface, move the data model to `Cow`, give
  `Measured` a sign, and implement §15.1 in the library.
- **§15.1 is `UNIMPLEMENTED`** for `ucal-body` (D-A20). The binary's loader is a
  partial implementation and says so.
- **A rounded parameter is a different calendar** (D-A21's neighbour finding):
  anyone authoring a body file decides their intercalation with the last digit
  they write.
- **W3, a third locale**, postponed with its reason and the decision it would
  force already recorded.
- **Titan and the four bodies added in 0.8.0 have no anchor**, for the reason
  D5's literature search established.
- **[`GE-U4-walk.sh`](../Proposals/GE-U4-walk.sh) is built and unrun.**
- **The signing key has no authority behind it that is not the author**, and the
  release checksums are not signed.
- **Eight commits in the history are unsigned**, permanently.
