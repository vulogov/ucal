# 1.5.0 — UNRELEASED

In development on the `1.5.0` branch. `main` carries the released 1.4.0 line.

## The goal of this cycle: spend what the research found

1.4.0 was a research cycle and it produced more than it built. Three findings
came with a recommendation attached, and none of them has been acted on:

- **W4 step 1** placed every unit of every body on the universal ladder and
  found Earth's day and Mars's sol on the same rung, 591 and 607, where the
  steps are a factor of 3125. Its recommendation was explicit: do not build the
  two-ladder view, **keep the table** — as a row or two in `ucal cal show`.
- **X1.3** landed a body-file loader and a `cal derive` that stops at
  intercalation, because §15.1's *other* file — the anchor — is still compiled
  in. A derived calendar without a phase cannot produce a date, which is the
  thing a calendar is for.
- **The event catalogue** doubled and now reaches T31. Seven derived calendars
  do not: the mechanism is body-independent and the catalogue of bodies is the
  thinnest part of the argument it makes.

None of this is new design. It is finishing what the last cycle established, and
the cycle is deliberately small.

### Scope

**Y1 — the ladder placement, in `cal show`.** Where this body's day and year sit
on the universal grid, and how far above the rung. W4's kept recommendation,
implemented at the size W4 recommended: rows, not a view.

**Y2 — anchor files.** §15.1 names body files *and* anchor files, versioned
independently because parameters change with better measurement and anchors with
re-determination. 1.4.0 did the first. With the second, `ucal cal derive
--anchor <file>` produces **local fields for a body that does not ship** — a
date, not just an intercalation rule.

This is where the temptation lives, and Rule J is the answer: an anchor is
determined and cited, never guessed. A file must carry the same obligations the
compiled-in anchors do — a phase definition that is a body event, a determination,
a citation, a window containing its own tick — and `Anchor::new` already refuses
anything less. The loader must not become the place that stops asking.

**Y3 — more bodies.** The Galilean moons and Enceladus, cited, most of them
landing where Titan is: complete in units, intercalation and cycles, incomplete
in phase. That is the ordinary case and a `cal list` that shows it eleven times
makes the argument better than one that shows it five.

### Done when

Y1 and Y2 have landed, and Y3 has landed or been recorded as dropped with a
reason. A body that does not ship can be given an anchor from a file and asked
for a date.

### Explicitly not in scope

- **Anything breaking.** `cargo semver-checks` runs against the published 1.4.0.
  The four items in [`ROAD-TO-2.0.md`](../Proposals/ROAD-TO-2.0.md) stay there.
- **Moving the loader into `ucal-body`.** That is 2.0's, for the reason D-A20
  gives: the data model is `&'static str` and a library loader would leak.
- **W3, the third locale**, postponed in 1.4.0 pending a choice of language,
  which decides what the probe tests.

## Breaking

_none, and none is permitted._

## Added

- **The ladder placement in `cal show`** (Y1). Three rows — this body's solar
  day, year and grouping cycle — each on the coarsest universal tier no longer
  than it, with how far above the rung it sits. Earth's day is `T1 arc 591.3`
  against §4.3's published 591.25; Mars's sol is 607.5, on the same rung, on a
  ladder whose steps are a factor of 3125. W4 step 1's kept recommendation, at
  the size it recommended.
- **Five bodies** (Y3): Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto and Enceladus. Nine
  calendars to fourteen, twelve of them derived. All tidally locked, so Titan's
  derivation became a shared `locked_moon`.
- **Anchor files** (Y2), §15.1's second file. `ucal cal derive <body> --anchor
  <anchor> --at <instant>` produces a **date** for a body that does not ship.
  `Meridian::new` and `Determination::new` in `ucal-body`, without which the
  format was not implementable from outside that crate at all.
- **A derived parameter in a body file** (Z1.1): `derived: synodic` computes the
  solar day exactly, in ticks, from the rotation and year the file already
  states.
- **`ucal wallclock`** — a full-screen clock, behind the non-default `tui`
  feature. Two themes: `plain`, and `startrek`, which is LCARS. `--locale ru`
  draws the tier names in Russian (Rule N); `--clock-local mars-d` puts a body's
  own calendar on the face as a second dial.
- **`ucal wallclock --once [--at <INSTANT>]`** — one frame to stdout, byte-for-byte
  reproducible, and therefore committable. Two frames in
  [`CLI-EXAMPLES.md`](../CLI-EXAMPLES.md) are generated by it and held current by
  `check-docs`.
- **Three palette themes**: `amber` (VT220), `green` (3270), and `paper`, which
  commits to a light background rather than inheriting the terminal's.
- **`--theme starwars`**, a targeting computer, and the third *layout*. Canopy
  brackets, a reticle round the beat, the flicker riding the crosshair, every
  other hand in one strip. `Theme` grew a `Layout` enum to hold it — `lcars:
  bool` was honest for exactly as long as there were two arrangements, which
  [`Z2-wallclock-faces.md`](../Proposals/Z2-wallclock-faces.md) predicted in as
  many words.
- **`UCAL-E0017`**, for a data file that cannot be read or is not well-formed
  (D-A22).
- **`xtask lint` gains `codes-have-raisers`** (D-A23).

## Changed

- **MSRV raised to 1.88**, from 1.87. Forced by the `tui` feature: `ratatui`
  reaches `instability` and `darling`, which declare 1.88. An MSRV increase is a
  minor-version change ([`STABILITY.md`](../STABILITY.md)) and this is the
  release note it requires.

  It was carried as a *stated exception* first — default configuration at 1.87,
  `--features tui` needing more — and that was the wrong shape. A feature that
  cannot be built at the declared MSRV is a second, unstated MSRV, and a caller
  told their toolchain is too old has not been given a number they could have
  checked. The MSRV job now builds `--features tui` as well as the workspace —
  not `--all-features`, which enables both backends and trips the guard that
  makes them exclusive.

  The library crates are unaffected in substance: their floor remains `bnum
  0.14`'s 1.87, and a caller depending on `ucal-core` alone needs no more.
- `cal derive`'s "no anchor" note counts the registry instead of stating a
  number. It said "five of the seven shipped calendars" and Y3 made that ten of
  twelve.
- The wall clock's second dial states what its year counts. See *Fixed*.

## Fixed

- **`Documentation/examples/europa.hjson` derived a different calendar from the
  body it names.** It stated a solar day of `3.552106` and cited the NASA fact
  sheet for it — a figure that source does not publish, wrong in the third
  decimal. `202/279` against the real body's `1/24`. It survived a full release
  cycle because the only check on it was that it parsed.
- **`UCAL-E0011` reported its own inverse** and was raised nowhere (D-A23). The
  collision branch in `locale::validate` raised `UCAL-E0014`, *name not found in
  the active locale table*, from 1.2.0 until now.
- **The body-file loader reported `UCAL-E0010`**, *locale table load failure*,
  for a body file (D-A22).
- **`--clock-local earth-d` showed a bare `year 27`**, which invites two wrong
  readings — "2027" and "2000 + 27" — off by one in opposite directions. It is
  the twenty-seventh year of a reckoning that starts at 1, and the face says so.

## Internal

- Workspace and all seven packages at `1.5.0`.
- **A known borrowed code, recorded rather than spot-fixed.** `wallclock
  --once`'s frame-size guard raises `UCAL-E0001`, declared as *malformed
  timestamp*, because that is what every other argument rejection in this binary
  already uses — `--color`, `--tick-sep`. It is the D-A22 class again, and the
  fix is a survey of argument validation and one code for it, not a fourth
  borrowed-code correction squeezed into this cycle. `codes-have-raisers` cannot
  find this shape and says so.
- The `features` workflow caught `tui` failing to declare `civil` and then
  failing to declare `body` — the same class of defect twice in one afternoon on
  one feature.

## Carried forward from 1.4.0

- **The three asks in [`CONTACT.md`](../CONTACT.md)** are open, wanted, and — as
  of 1.4.0 — a condition on nothing shipping.
- **The 2.0 bundle**, decided in `ROAD-TO-2.0.md` and not scheduled: withdraw
  `bigint` from the public feature surface, move the data model to `Cow`, give
  `Measured` a sign, and implement §15.1 in the library.
- **§15.1 is `UNIMPLEMENTED`** for `ucal-body` (D-A20). The binary's loader is a
  partial implementation and says so.
- **A rounded parameter is a different calendar** (D-A21's neighbour finding):
  anyone authoring a body file decides their intercalation with the last digit
  they write.
- **W3, a third locale**, postponed with its reason and the decision it would
  force already recorded.
- **Titan and the four bodies added in 0.8.0 have no anchor**, for the reason
  D5's literature search established.
- **[`GE-U4-walk.sh`](../Proposals/GE-U4-walk.sh) is built and unrun.**
- **The signing key has no authority behind it that is not the author**, and the
  release checksums are not signed.
- **Eight commits in the history are unsigned**, permanently.
