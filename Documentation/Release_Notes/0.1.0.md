# 0.1.0 — 2026-07-31

First release. RFC UCAL-1 implemented in full, phases UC-P0 through UC-P16.

Six crates published to crates.io: `ucal-core`, `ucal-civil`, `ucal-body`,
`ucal-events`, `ucal-cosmo`, `ucal`.

## Added

**`ucal-core`** — the tick, the base-5 tier grid, and the value types.

- `Instant`, `Delta`, `Window`, `Span`, `Stated`, and the inert `Signed` /
  `SignedWindow` pair that carries a signed quantity without admitting it to
  arithmetic.
- The tier ladder `5^(5k)`, indexed on the beat at `5^60`. Tier names are
  display aliases in `en` and `ru`; nothing decides behaviour from a name.
- Both text forms — the human form anchored at T0, the `digit5` form anchored
  at T32 — the fixed 64-byte binary form, and 52-character UCIDs.
- Exact arithmetic: `Ratio`, `RatInterval`, directed integer square roots,
  continued fractions and convergents, checked `mul_div`.
- Two integer backends behind one trait: `bnum::U512` by default, and
  `num_bigint::BigUint` under `--features bigint`.

**`ucal-civil`** — the SI bridge, and the only door to Earth's civil labels.

- Exact TT pivot, TAI, and UTC with the IERS leap-second table.
- The 1961–1972 rubber-second era, exactly representable: every rate
  coefficient divides by 27, which cancels 86400's factor of 3³.
- Gregorian and Julian, both labelled *legacy*.

**`ucal-body`** — cited body parameters, and calendars derived from them.

- Earth, Mars and Titan, each parameter recorded as published with its
  citation and provenance.
- One generic derivation path with no per-body branch. Intercalation comes out
  of continued-fraction expansion: Earth reaches 31/128 and Mars 45/76 from the
  same drift bound, stated in each body's own local days and years.

**`ucal-events`** — interval-valued, cited milestones, each warned about when it
falls inside the datum's own claim half-width.

**`ucal-cosmo`** — flat ΛCDM, age from redshift and back, by certified integer
interval quadrature. No float and no transcendental anywhere. Every result
carries two widths that are never merged.

**`ucal`** — the command line: `now`, `datum`, `explain`, `from-civil`,
`to-civil`, `ladder`, `cal`, `show`, `events`, `timeline`, `ruler`, `cosmo`,
`doctor`. `--json` on all of them.

## Verification

The RFC was checked before it was implemented, by independent exact-integer
computation along two routes. Every Appendix A constant, the whole §2.2
provenance chain, all eight Appendix C tick fixtures, the Appendix I
intercalation derivations and the §2.4 alignment invariants reproduce
bit-exactly.

Fifteen entries came out of that pass — fourteen standing deltas and one
withdrawal — recorded in [`spec/SPEC-DELTAS.md`](../../spec/SPEC-DELTAS.md),
each covered by a test. The most consequential:

- **D-A12** — §9.6's synodic formula measures against the primary's *solar day*,
  but Appendix I.2 uses the *year*. For Earth those are 1.038 d and 29.53 d.
  Only the year-relative form reproduces I.2.
- **D-A14** — §10.3's cosmological integral is improper and cannot be
  quadratured as written. The substitution `u = 1/(1+z)` makes the range
  compact; without it, "certified" would mean "certified apart from the tail".
- **D-A1** — **withdrawn.** A claimed error in the RFC's 44 BC fixture that
  turned out, on a second look, to be an error in the oracle. Appendix C is
  8/8 correct.

The six §21 gated experiments were run rather than assumed. **Four kill criteria
fired**, and the outcomes are recorded rather than quietly worked around:

| | outcome |
|---|---|
| GE-1 | **fired** — depth-24 quadrature is hours, not seconds. Default depth 12, `--depth` is the high-precision mode |
| GE-2 | **fired** — a ≤ 1-tick enclosure at `z = 1100` is unreachable by ~55 orders of magnitude. Width published, `UCAL-W0004` always set |
| GE-3 | answered — Earth ±1 ms, Mars ±1 s, Titan **none**: no convention exists to adopt, and inventing one is what GE-3 forbids |
| GE-4 | **fired on arrival** — bnum 0.14 changed its API mid-implementation. All of it absorbed in `TickInt` |
| GE-5 | green — `ucal-core` builds `no_std` with no allocator for wasm |
| GE-6 | **fired** — a CMB-anchored datum is ~10× wider than the published one. D-21 stands |

376 tests green on both backends. `cargo run -p xtask` reproduces the constants
along two independent routes, 96/96.

## Known limits

- **The API is not stable.** A `0.x` bump may break it.
- **Cosmological enclosures are wide**, and honestly so — thousands of years at
  recombination. The binding constraint is Planck's own error bars, not the
  arithmetic; see GE-1 above.
- **Titan has no anchor**, so `titan-d` is a calendar complete in structure and
  unusable for dating. That is the correct state, not a gap to be filled by
  assumption.
- **UTC before 1961** is refused. There is nothing to convert exactly.
