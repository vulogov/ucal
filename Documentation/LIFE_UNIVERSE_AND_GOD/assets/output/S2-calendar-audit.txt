GE-A2 asked whether this audit produces a non-trivial result — the kill
criterion being that every historical calendar turns out to be a
convergent, which would make the finding empty. It does not: the split
is real, and it does not follow accuracy. The Persian rule of 1079 is
derived and the Gregorian reform of 1582 is not.
